# Tool watch 2026-11

**Verdict: maybe.** Eleven v4 (late Sept/early Oct 2026) is a credible, cheap way to test directed voice acting now, but nothing found fixes the performance-transfer or painterly-motion questions with evidence on masks. Cheap voice test only; do not restart the full pass.

Caveat: this was the first report (no earlier ones in `animation/tool-watch/`). The session could not fetch primary pages (elevenlabs.io, runway.com, openrouter.ai and kombitz.com failed DNS, so they need allowing in the session's network settings). Everything below comes from web-search summaries and third-party sites, and is unverified against vendor pages.

## 1. Voice acting
- **ElevenLabs Eleven v4** (reported 28 Sep or 7 Oct 2026; sources disagree). Follows inline audio tags and direction prompts ("[said angrily]") more accurately than v3; 90+ languages, multi-speaker dialogue, Professional Voice Clone, 10,000 characters per request. List price about $0.08 per 1,000 characters (roughly a minute of speech); the $0.022 launch price ended 12 Oct. **v4 Turbo** about $0.04 per 1K, built for low latency. Why it matters: it is the "ElevenLabs v3 class" option from the retro, now better at acting notes, and a designed or cloned voice can be reused across all 23+ lines. Use v4, not Turbo, for acting quality.
- **OpenAI**: gpt-realtime-2 (7 May 2026; a 2.1 and mini reportedly July, unconfirmed) are agent voices; no dedicated acting-direction TTS found. Nothing new that fixes gpt-4o-mini-tts.
- Rough-reading-to-performance (speech-to-speech): ElevenLabs has a voice changer, but I found no v4-specific speech-to-speech details or pricing. Worth a hands-on check, since it is the one route that keeps an amateur's timing.
- Not checked in depth: Hume, Cartesia, Fish Audio, Google, open models.

## 2. Talking heads, lip sync, performance transfer
- **Runway Act-Two**: still the performance-transfer route. Help docs say it handles non-human characters and styles; a Sept 2026 third-party guide says it works on illustrations, puppets and 3D renders but needs clearly defined eyes and a mouth (a mask with fixed eye holes may or may not qualify). Singles subject, face visible, framed waist-up at widest. Changelog mentions a voice-change option inside Act-Two. No dated 2026 Act-Two upgrade confirmed; price not retrieved. Runway Characters (9 Mar 2026) is a real-time avatar API, not relevant for film.
- **Kling Avatar v2** (Standard/Pro): guide dated 22 Oct recommends it for photo-plus-audio clips. Prices vary by host: roughly $0.036 to $0.056 per second Standard, $0.068 to $0.112 Pro (Hedra, reseller, fal figures), so about $0.20 to $0.70 for a 6 s line against 24 credits last time.
- **Hedra Character-3** (merged Avatar/Omnia, July 2026): about 6 credits per second on one tracker; vendor claims stylised/non-human support, no independent mask tests.
- **OmniHuman v1.5/2.0 (ByteDance)**, **SkyReels V3 Talking Avatar** (720p, up to 20 s), **Sync Lipsync 3 Avatar**, **MuseTalk** (open, needs GPU): listed in guides; no evidence on masks or creatures.
- Verdict: no tool proven on ape masks beyond Kling Avatar. A one-shot Act-Two test on the Major plate, driven by a phone clip of the owner, remains the best next experiment.

## 3. Style-locked image-to-video and image generators
- No image-to-video model found that is documented to hold a painterly surface in motion. Kling Restyle remains the fallback named in the retro.
- **Kandinsky 6.0 Video** (open weights, MIT, 6 Oct 2026; 29B Pro and 3B Lite, 5 s clips): nothing found on style retention. **Wan 3.0**: marketed on subject consistency; dates conflict.
- **Anima Reference Adapter** (pilot, 27 Oct): image-only; reported weak transfer of painterly artist styles.
- **Midjourney** V8 line (V8.2 default reported 24 Jul 2026): `--sref` carries palette, texture and lighting but not characters. **Nano Banana 2** (Gemini 3.1 Flash Image, Feb 2026): up to 4K, API, reported stable with style references. Neither tested head to head on painterly lock; try the page-crop reference test already planned (lock block v3) first, since the drift was a prompt problem.
